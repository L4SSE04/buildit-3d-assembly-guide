Who does what the split between the AI model and our own code

Group 38 · November 2026

The AI model does

  • Reads the drawings. Identifies parts and counts from the parts page, follows the step order, and guesses where each board goes and which way it faces. Output: the guide as JSON in our schema.
  • Repairs on request. Gets a numbered problem list from our checker and answers with an edit list ({"edits":[{"panel":"B1","set":{…}}]}). It never gets a free hand: we apply the edits and re-check.
  • Compares pictures. Looks at our 3D renders next to the manual pages and points out mismatches, again as an edit list or NO_CHANGES.
  • Answers one question about one step in the "Stuck on this step?" box.

Everything the model returns is treated as a proposal. A change is kept only if our check scores it better (or, for the compare pass, not worse).

Our code does (deterministic, testable)

  • Geometry representation. Millimetre boxes plus primitives (cylinder, plate, lathe, tube, sweep) for curved parts; explicit joints (attach: {to, face, x/y/z}) and hardware rows (at + to + n) that normalizeGuide resolves into exact coordinates.
  • Collision and gap detection. collide.js samples each part's surface and runs a signed-distance test against the others; works for rotated and curved parts; declared overlaps (pole in socket) are allowed.
  • Assembly-consistency rules. check.js: floating boards, part and hardware counts vs. the parts list, hardware off its board or on the wrong face, dowels and cam screws that point into no board, cam screw ↔ cam lock pairing, nails that miss the board behind, steps that forget hardware they use.
  • Automatic corrections. autofix.js nudges parts out of small overlaps, places unpaired cam locks, re-aims screws; each fix kept only if the score improves.
  • Rendering and the viewer. Keyframes, staggered timing, auto camera, labels, part models with real hole patterns, actual-size SVG profiles; the same renderer makes the pictures for the compare pass.
  • Evaluation. scripts/eval.mjs runs the test manuals through the pipeline and reports errors, warnings, cost and time per manual; xcheck.mjs puts each manual page next to the 3D view for human scoring.
